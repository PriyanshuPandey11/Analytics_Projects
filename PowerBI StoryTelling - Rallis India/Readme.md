# Power BI Storytelling – Rallis India

## Overview

**Power BI Storytelling – Rallis India** is an end-to-end Business Intelligence project developed in collaboration with **industry experts and professionals from Rallis India**.

As part of a cross-functional team, I contributed to transforming real-world inventory data into actionable business insights using Power BI. The project covered the complete analytics lifecycle, including business problem identification, data modeling, dashboard development, analysis, and presentation.

The project follows a structured storytelling approach to answer three key questions:

* What happened?
* Why did it happen?
* What should be done next?

## Business Problem

The project focused on inventory management challenges affecting operational efficiency and working capital, including:

* Inventory movement delays
* Unserved stock across warehouses
* Slow-moving and ageing inventory
* Inventory nearing expiry
* Excess inventory and blocked working capital

The objective was to identify key inventory issues, understand their root causes, and provide business-ready insights for better decision-making.

## Tools & Technologies

* Power BI
* DAX
* Power Query
* Data Modeling
* Drill-down & Drill-through Analysis
* Decomposition Tree
* Interactive Data Visualization

## Dashboard Overview

The project consists of **three interactive dashboards**:

### 1. Executive Overview

Provides a consolidated view of key inventory KPIs and overall inventory health for management.

### 2. Inventory Performance Analysis

Analyzes inventory delays, unserved stock, slow-moving inventory, ageing stock, product performance, and category-level trends.

### 3. Root Cause Analysis

Uses advanced Power BI capabilities, including the **Decomposition Tree**, to identify the key drivers behind inventory issues and support actionable recommendations.
